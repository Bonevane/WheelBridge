The first public build of WheelBridge: force feedback for Logitech wheels in Windows racing games running under Wine on a Mac.

## What's in it

- **Force feedback in any DirectInput game.** A small `dinput8.dll` in the bottle shows games a G29 with every DirectInput effect: constant, periodic, ramp, spring, damper, friction and inertia. The helper works out the torque at 500 Hz.
- **32-bit and 64-bit games**, from one install per bottle.
- **CrossOver, Whisky, Sikarugir, Kegworks, Wineskin, Heroic, Game Porting Toolkit and plain Wine.** Prefixes are found on their own, and Add Wine Prefix… covers the rest. If you have more than one CrossOver, pick which one in Bottles.
- **Classic Logitech wheels:** G29, G923 for PlayStation, G27, G25, Driving Force GT, Driving Force Pro, Driving Force and EX, MOMO Force and MOMO Racing. Wheels that start in compatibility mode are switched to their own mode.
- **Safe by default.** The motor is released when the game stalls, pauses or quits, when the helper stops, and when the wheel is unplugged. Stop Motor in the menu bar releases it at once.
- **Clean installs.** Everything changed in a bottle is backed up, and uninstall puts it back byte for byte. When a CrossOver update replaces Wine's DirectInput, Repair fixes the bottle in one click.
- **Updates from GitHub.** WheelBridge checks for new releases once a day, and downloads and installs them when you say so.

## Tested

Only the G29 in F1 2021 under CrossOver has been tried on real hardware. Everything else follows published protocol notes and passes the test suite, so reports from other wheels, games and launchers are very welcome. See [SUPPORT.md](https://github.com/Bonevane/WheelBridge/blob/main/docs/SUPPORT.md).

## Not supported yet

The G920, the Xbox G923 and the PRO Racing Wheel are recognised but not driven. TrueForce, Thrustmaster and Fanatec aren't supported.

## Install

1. Download `WheelBridge-0.1.0.dmg`, open it and drag WheelBridge onto Applications.
2. Open WheelBridge. It isn't notarized by Apple yet, so the first time macOS refuses. Click **Done**, then go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to WheelBridge.
3. Allow Input Monitoring when macOS asks.
4. Install into your bottles from the Bottles screen.

Later versions install themselves from Health › Updates.
